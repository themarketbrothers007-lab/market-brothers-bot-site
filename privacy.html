Privacy Policy — Market Brothers Bot

Last updated: October 2026

Market Brothers Bot is a personal automation tool that connects to the Market Brothers YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API to upload video content prepared by the channel owner.

Data accessed: This application requests YouTube account access solely to upload videos and manage them on the Market Brothers channel. It does not access, store, or share any other Google account data.

Data sharing: No data accessed through this application is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Data retention: Authorization tokens are stored locally and used only to maintain the upload connection. They can be revoked at any time via Google Account security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
